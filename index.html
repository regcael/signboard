  1. Message us on WhatsApp and tell us which package you want.

Short terms

  1. What you get: a one-page website for your business, with hosting included and no monthly fee.
  2. Fee: as listed for your package. 50% to start and 50% before the page goes live. The first 50% is non-refundable once the build starts. By making payment, you agree to these terms of service and privacy policy.
  3. Content: a short welcome line, your social media bio if you have one, and posts from one of your social media accounts shown on the page. We don't copy or download your photos.
  4. Approval: you approve the page before it goes live.
  5. Changes: our involvement ends at handover. Later changes are a separate quote.
  6. Ownership: the website and its hosting stay with SignBoard. Your content stays yours. Moving the site to another host is a separate build, by SignBoard or by someone you hire.
  7. Address: the site uses a free web address. A custom domain is available upon request, charges will be quoted separate as per the domain name provider registered by SignBoard and renewed yearly at the provider’s price.
  8. No promised results: the page is a professional presence for your business and doesn't promise enquiries or sales. It shows your social media posts, so if you make your account private or delete posts, they won't appear on the page.
  9. Communication: by email after payment, with a reply within one working day. Our emails will come from firstreginacaeli@gmail.com.

Privacy

  1. What we collect: the details you share so we can build your page: your name, business name, contact details, the link to your social media account, and payment confirmations.
  2. How we use it: only to talk to you, build and host your page, and keep payment records. We don't sell your details or share them for advertising.
  3. Your social media posts: your page shows posts from one of your own accounts. We don't copy or download your photos. They stay on your account.
  4. Other services involved: messages before payment go through WhatsApp, which has its own privacy policy. This page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, and your page is stored with a hosting provider.
  5. Keeping and deleting: we keep your details only as long as needed to deliver your page and for our records. To ask us to delete your details, message us on WhatsApp or email us.
  6. This website: it has no sign-up, no tracking and no analytics.
